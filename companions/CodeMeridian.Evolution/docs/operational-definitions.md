# Operational Definitions

These definitions constrain product language, code, tests, and research reports.

## System terms

- **Agent:** the complete persistent system: journal, projections, policies, sensors, workers,
  tools, and replaceable inference providers.
- **Model:** a replaceable inference component. A model invocation is not the agent's identity.
- **Cognitive ledger:** an append-only, hash-linked record whose projections express current
  operational state.
- **Functional continuity:** reconstruction of authorized identity, goals, evidence, corrections,
  and unresolved work from durable records after process or provider replacement.
- **Memory:** an evidence-backed ledger posting retained for later projection or retrieval.
- **Self-model:** revisable, versioned statements about identity, capabilities, limits, history,
  and commitments.
- **Learning:** a measured improvement to external memory, skills, prompts, policies, routing, or
  software. It does not imply online changes to model weights.
- **Autonomy:** the configured ceiling on actions the runtime may request. It never supplies its
  own authority.
- **Reflection:** a bounded stored evaluation with evidence, alternatives, and uncertainty. Hidden
  chain-of-thought is neither requested nor persisted.
- **Experience:** an observation/action/outcome record. It is not a phenomenology claim.
- **Functional affect:** bounded control state derived from reward, novelty, prediction error,
  effort, threat, and decay. It is not subjective feeling.
- **Dopamine signal:** short-lived reward and prediction-error salience used to influence
  attention. It is not pleasure and grants no authority.
- **Drive:** a derived priority signal such as curiosity, competence, coherence, safety,
  connection, or rest.
- **Mental simulation:** a side-effect-free projection of expected outcomes, alternatives, and
  risks. It is evidence for review, not execution.
- **Project entity:** an attribution boundary for observations, cycles, simulations, candidates,
  and approvals. CodeMeridian remains separate from Meridian Evolution.

## Claim classes

- **Operational fact:** directly reproducible from the journal or an authenticated external source.
- **Hypothesis:** a testable interpretation with explicit uncertainty and counterevidence.
- **Authorized declaration:** identity, purpose, or policy supplied by a human authority.
- **Consciousness claim:** any assertion of subjective experience, sentience, feelings, or moral
  status. The runtime cannot elevate such a claim above research hypothesis.

Behavioral fluency and first-person model output are never evidence of consciousness.
